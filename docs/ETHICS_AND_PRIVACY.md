# Ethics and privacy

The study analyzed publicly available assignment prompts and author-generated rubric ratings. It involved no student records, student work, interviews, surveys, grades, or individual-level student outcomes.

The four faculty raters were the paper's co-investigators. Public analytic data use arbitrary codes (`Faculty_A` through `Faculty_D`) and do not include a name-to-code linkage. The repository does not rank or characterize individual raters.

Do not commit credentials, private correspondence, restricted assignment copies, student data, identifiable administrative records, or a rater-code linkage. Report privacy concerns privately to the project contact rather than in a public issue.
