Prompt used

The above PowerPoint contains our framework to evaluate assignments. Evaluate the following assignment using the rubric discussed in the PowerPoint. (Attached the PowerPoint used in TLT)

Evaluation: CYBER 301 Final Project

1️⃣ Real-World Relevance → 3 (Strong)

✅ Designing a network security plan for a small business is highly authentic
✅ Includes realistic components (IDS, firewall, risk matrix, budget)
✅ Mirrors actual industry tasks

Why not lower? This closely resembles professional cybersecurity consulting work.

2️⃣ Complexity & Critical Thinking → 3 (Strong)

✅ Requires system-level thinking
✅ Integrates architecture, policy, technical controls, and budgeting
✅ Students must synthesize multiple cybersecurity domains

This is cognitively demanding and multi-layered.

3️⃣ Open-Endedness / Student Agency → 2 (Moderate)

✅ Students design their own plan
✅ Multiple possible correct solutions

⚠️ However:

Scenario is generic (“a small business”)

No stakeholder constraints, industry type, or evolving conditions

No required tradeoff justification beyond budget

Could be stronger with contextual variability.

4️⃣ Authentic Evaluation Criteria → 2 (Moderate)

Current grading focuses on:

Completeness (40%)

Technical accuracy (30%)

Presentation (20%)

Budget (10%)

⚠️ Rubric emphasizes product quality, not:

Risk reasoning transparency

Tradeoff decisions

Justification of design choices

Adaptability or threat modeling rigor

Evaluation is somewhat traditional.

5️⃣ AI / Academic Integrity Vulnerability → 1 (Weak / High Risk)

⚠️ Highly vulnerable to AI generation:

Generic security plans are easily produced by AI

Budget justifications can be generated

Policy templates are widely available

No required contextual data unique to class

Without checkpoints or personalization, AI can produce most of this.

📊 Overall Score

3 + 3 + 2 + 2 + 1 = 11 / 15

✅ Strong real-world alignment
⚠️ Moderate authenticity
⚠️ High AI vulnerability

🚨 Biggest Vulnerability

AI-generability due to generic scenario and product-only submission.

Students can prompt:
“Create a network security plan for a small business including firewall, IDS, risk matrix…”

And receive a near-complete draft.

🔧 One High-Impact Redesign Improvement

Add a “Dynamic Constraint & Defense” Component

Require students to:

Select or be assigned a specific industry (e.g., healthcare clinic, retail chain, law firm)

Respond to a mid-project “incident injection” (e.g., ransomware attack, insider threat, budget cut)

Submit a short recorded defense explaining:

Why they chose specific controls

What tradeoffs they made

What they deliberately excluded and why

This: ✅ Reduces AI substitution
✅ Increases reasoning transparency
✅ Enhances authenticity
✅ Strengthens evaluation of decision-making

✅ Addresses counterarguments

No requirement for original contribution

No multi-modal or situational complexity

✅ Multiple defensible theses possible

✅ Wide intellectual flexibility

MLA format

Impact or persuasive effectiveness
